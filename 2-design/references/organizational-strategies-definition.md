# Orgnizational strategies definitions

**Introduction**: The introduction prepares learners to learn by motivating them, explaining what they will learn, and activating relevant prior knowledge.
**Content Presentation and Learning** : This phase delivers and teaches the content using instructional strategies that align with the learning objectives and the learners’ required level of knowledge or skill.
**Assessment**: The assessment phase measures whether learners have achieved the intended learning objectives through aligned methods such as tests, projects, or assignments.
**Utilization**: The utilization phase supports retention and transfer by giving learners opportunities and resources to apply what they learned in practical or real-world contexts.
