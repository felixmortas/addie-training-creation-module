# Select strategies and finalize the design handoff

Read `assets/strategy-catalogue.md` and `assets/organizational-and-delivery-strategies-template.md`. For each task, identify its learning domain (cognitive, affective, psychomotor), then select only catalogue strategies that fit the domain, knowledge level, learner/context evidence, and project constraints. A hybrid must be explicitly named and derived from named catalogue strategies.

Choose an organizational approach that sequences introduction, content presentation and learning, assessment, and utilization; use Gagné's nine events as the default unless the selected evidence supports another catalogued approach. Specify delivery strategies for the relevant phases, including transfer or utilization where the task requires it. Do not confuse a development method with an organizational or delivery decision.

Create `<course-folder>/design/organizational-and-delivery-strategies.md` from the template. Its required alignment table has exactly these columns:

| Task | Learning Objective | Knowledge level | Assessment |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

Include every in-scope task once. Below the table, document the organizational approach and task-specific delivery strategy selections, with a short evidence-based rationale and any assumptions or open gaps. Confirm that each table row is aligned and that all choices use the approved catalogues.

Ask for approval. On approval, mark the artifact `approved` and report its path, selected upstream inputs, unresolved risks, and that `/addie-develop` should receive this approved handoff plus the selected analysis evidence. If approval is withheld, stop without representing the design as approved.
