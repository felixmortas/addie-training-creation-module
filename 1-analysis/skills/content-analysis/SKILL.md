The content analysis is usually a short session where you sit with the SME and go through the client’s content or it may even involve you looking at documents/materials. The goal is to determine how much content needs to be included in this training so you can come up with a budget/schedule.

> "The client will tell you it's a 30-minute course. Then you walk through the content and it's 2 hours."

Without this, your budget and schedule are fiction.
