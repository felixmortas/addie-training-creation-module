The learner analysis seeks to determine who the learners are that are going to be going through your training.
For example, you are going to try to determine: Who are the learners? What is their level of education? Can they use computers? Can they use smartphones? What is their age? What are their motivations? What are their work conditions like? Are they highly-skilled surgeons or are you training a new computer analyst? And do either of those people have time to take your training?

Does the audience prefer a certain kind of training? Are they used to online courses? Are they more comfortable sitting in a classroom?

The point here is: Make sure you know who your learners are going to be so that your solution meets their needs.

Note: There's no research to show learning styles actually exist, so we're not looking for that in our learner analysis. We might look at what the learner preference is, but those are not the same thing. There is no learning style. We might examine if the learners prefer and are used to learning via video, online learning, computer-based training, classroom, etc
