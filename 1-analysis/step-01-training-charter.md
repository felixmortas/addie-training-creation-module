# Establish the project

Create or update `{analysis_output_folder}/training-charter.md` from `assets/training-charter-template.md`. Treat it as the maintained project record, not a promise that training is the answer.

Capture the client, business problem, observed symptoms, suspected causes, stakeholders and roles, SME access, constraints, current content, delivery environment known so far, scope boundaries, budget, schedule, risks, success/ROI hypotheses, and unanswered questions. Mark unknowns plainly. If source materials are available, perform a content walkthrough now; use its estimated content volume to challenge unrealistic budget or schedule expectations.

Confirm with the user that the charter accurately represents the kickoff context. Then read fully and follow `[[bmad-snapshot:step-02-need-analysis.md]]`.
