## INSTRUCTIONS

Create goals

There are two kinds of goals. 
The first one is a project goal (think project management goals), that is related to the overall project.
The other type of goals are our instructional goals. These are our
goals for instruction.

- **Goal vs Objective:** goal is general ("train users to fly a plane"); objective is specific and measurable.
To illustrate this in an example - my goal is that the learner will be able to perform a needs analysis. An objective for this goal could be, given xyz case scenario, the learner will be able to conduct a learner analysis. The objective is much more specific. You could even get even more specific.
You can write many objectives based on that one goal.
